# RTX-UNLOCK local files

Keep DEBUG.ON for logs. This port always requires the physical U confirmation.
Esc or 30 seconds without confirmation cancels; cancellation does not start Windows.
A successful attempt may take about 25 seconds from U to Windows on the verified board.

Generate the five firmware assets locally into ASSETS using Fetch-Firmware.cmd.
Do not use another machine's PROFILE.BIN or PROM. Profiles and RUNxx logs are local.
No NVIDIA firmware or captured PROM is distributed in the public ZIP.
